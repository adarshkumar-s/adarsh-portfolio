ABOUT

Building, testing, and learning by making.

I like taking an idea far enough that it becomes something you can actually use—then looking closely at what worked, what didn't, and why.

PROFILE

From interfaces to the systems behind them.

The browser is usually where I start. From there, I follow the problem — through interaction, APIs, Python and small experiments — until an idea becomes something that actually works.

BUILDING

Useful before impressive

I prefer features that solve a real annoyance, even when the solution is smaller than the idea that started it.

INTERACTION

Details should respond

Motion, pointer behaviour and feedback are part of the interface—not decoration added after the useful part is finished.

EXPERIMENTS

Learn by pushing the browser

The Labs are a place to isolate one interaction, change a variable, and see what the browser actually does.

PROCESS

Keep the reasoning visible

I value understandable structure, honest limitations and code that can be revisited without having to rediscover the whole system.
