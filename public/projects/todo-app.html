TaskFlow

A focused, local-first task manager built to make everyday planning fast and frictionless.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptlocalStorage

Overview

TaskFlow lets you capture tasks, set priorities and due dates, search your list, filter by status, sort your workload and edit or remove tasks without an account.

Implementation

The app is fully client-side and uses localStorage for persistence. Task titles are rendered through DOM APIs rather than unsafe HTML insertion, with responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly controls and reduced-motion support.

Features

  • Create, complete, edit and delete tasks.
  • High, normal and low priorities plus optional due dates.
  • Instant search, All / Active / Completed filters and five sorting modes.
  • Complete all and clear completed bulk actions.
  • Persistent browser storage with no account or backend required.
