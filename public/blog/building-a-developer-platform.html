Building a developer platform instead of a portfolio page

A design and architecture note

One page can introduce you. A platform can show how you think.

Separate the surfaces

Projects answer what you built. Labs answer what you are exploring. Tools answer what you can make useful. Blog answers how you think about it. Stats answer what can be verified.

Keep claims proportional to evidence

A polished site should not require invented metrics, clients or job history. Missing information is better represented as a placeholder than as marketing copy that cannot be verified.

Make small things excellent

A Todo app, formatter or color utility can be valuable when its interaction is clear, its failure modes are handled and its implementation is understandable.

Leave room to grow

The architecture keeps content pages simple. New projects, labs and articles can be added without redesigning the whole platform.
