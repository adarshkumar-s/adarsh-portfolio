LAB / EXPERIMENT 01

Liquid Interaction.

A study of how pointer velocity can become physical-looking feedback. Move quickly and the surface pushes back; stop and it settles.

READY / MOVE POINTERMove across the A · touch supported
WHAT'S HAPPENING

Movement becomes distortion.

The experiment keeps the source image sharp, then offsets sampled image bands around the pointer. The response is interpolated and allowed to decay, so the surface feels disturbed rather than dragged.
