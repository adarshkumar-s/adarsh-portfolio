Liquid Interaction.

An experiment exploring how pointer movement can create fluid visual distortion. Faster movement adds energy; stillness lets the surface settle.

WHAT'S HAPPENING

Velocity becomes distortion.

The canvas keeps a sharp source image, then offsets sampled image bands around the pointer. Motion is interpolated and decays instead of snapping.
