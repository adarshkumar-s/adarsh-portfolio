LAB / INTERACTIVE 03

Scroll Playground.

A practical study of what scroll-linked motion can add without taking control away from the browser.

01 / REVEAL

Arrive gently.

Elements enter when they become relevant instead of animating the whole page on load.

02 / DEPTH

Keep movement small.

Transform and opacity suggest depth without moving the document underneath the reader.

03 / REST

Let the browser breathe.

The demo observes visibility and does animation work only while the experiment is actually in view.

WHAT'S HAPPENING

Scroll without taking control.

IntersectionObserver handles entry states. A small requestAnimationFrame loop updates the progress indicator only while the demo is visible, leaving native scrolling untouched.
