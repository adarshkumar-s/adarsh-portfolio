BUILD / TEST / REFINE03 PRINCIPLES

I prefer to make the smallest useful thing first, then let real behavior tell me what deserves another layer. The work moves between interface details, browser primitives and the practical systems underneath them.

01Find the constraint before choosing the tool.Start with what the interface actually needs, not what the stack makes fashionable.
02Let the browser carry its share.Use native behavior and platform capabilities before adding another abstraction.
03Make complexity explain itself.If an interaction or feature cannot justify its weight, it probably needs another pass.
